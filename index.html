FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The scanner isn't recognizing my card. What should I do?

Make sure the card is well-lit, flat, and out of any sleeve or binder page with glare. Hold the camera steady and centered on the card. If recognition still fails, use the "Search manually" button to find the card by name — this works for every card in our database.

Why does my free plan show a daily scan limit?

Free accounts include 20 scans per day, which resets at midnight in your local time zone. Pro subscribers get unlimited scans. Cards already in your collection don't count toward this limit when viewing them.

How accurate is the pricing data?

Prices are sourced from TCGPlayer market data and updated periodically. They reflect general market trends but can vary based on card condition, grading, and real-time marketplace activity. Use them as a guide rather than a guaranteed sale price.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select Pokescan, and choose "Cancel Subscription". Access continues until the end of your current billing period.

Can I transfer my collection to a new phone?

Yes — your collection is synced to your account via the cloud. Simply log in with the same account on your new device and your collection will sync automatically.

I was charged but didn't mean to subscribe. Can I get a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow the steps for your purchase. We're unable to issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help troubleshoot any issue with the app itself.

Is Pokescan affiliated with Pokémon or Nintendo?

No. Pokescan is an independent, unofficial app built by collectors for collectors. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures Inc., or Game Freak.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account within the app, or email privacy@pokescan.app and we'll process your request within 30 days.
